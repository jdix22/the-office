The signature course

Blur the Lines.

There is no one size fits all barber course. We meet you first and get to know who you are, then tailor two full days around your personality and skill set. Where you need more training, that is exactly what you get.

Level one

Fundamentals

  • Shape, balance and symmetry
  • Traditional to modern cuts: the foundation of fading, shaping and outlining
  • Bridging barbering and hairstyling, finishing included
  • Building your brand and social media presence

Level two

Intermediate

  • Advanced scissor techniques: texture, blending, finishing
  • Detailing your canvas: beard sculpting, face framing, fading
  • Incorporating colour: semi gloss, demi and permanent
  • Luxury steam shaving

Level three

Advanced

  • Creative cutting: hair designs, colour enhancements, portraits
  • Competition prep: performing under pressure with speed
  • Breaking the rules: avant garde styles and editorial looks
  • Creating content: tutorials, photo shoots and brand marketing

Who is this course for?

  • New and aspiring barbers and stylists ready for the next step
  • Working barbers who want to sharpen their craft
  • Creative professionals moving their skills into hair
  • Competitors preparing for the stage
  • Parents who want to cut, fade and style their kids’ hair
  • Anyone curious about the art of barbering
  • Anyone starting a fun, creative and lucrative career
