Scalp Micropigmentation

A fuller look, without surgery.

SMP uses fine needles to place tiny dots of pigment in the scalp, matching the look of natural hair growth. It is non-surgical, works for men and women, and can restore a hairline, fill thinning areas or cover scars.

  1. Consultation

    We assess your scalp and hair loss pattern, talk through your goals and design your new hairline together.

  2. Treatment sessions

    Pigment is layered over a few sessions, with colour and density adjusted to look natural on you.

  3. Aftercare

    Clear instructions to protect your result: mild shampoo, no direct sun, and no scratching while it settles.

The Office Academy

The most complete barber education.

Blur the Lines is a two day course built around you, taught by Benson and Victoria, our master stylists. Come ready to learn, build relationships and get better every day.

Level one

Fundamentals

  • Shape, balance and symmetry
  • Traditional to modern cuts: fading, shaping and outlining
  • Bridging barbering and hairstyling
  • Building your brand on social media

Level two

Intermediate

  • Advanced scissor work: texture, blending, finishing
  • Beard sculpting, face framing and fading
  • Working with colour: semi, demi and permanent
  • Luxury steam shaving

Level three

Advanced

  • Creative cutting: designs, colour and portraits
  • Competition prep: speed under pressure
  • Breaking the rules: avant garde and editorial looks
  • Creating content: tutorials, shoots and brand work
