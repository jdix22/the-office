Hair loss, handled with care.

Scalp micropigmentation places tiny dots of pigment in the scalp to recreate the look of natural hair. It is non-surgical, it works for men and women, and it starts with a private consultation.

What is SMP?

The look of a full head of hair, placed by hand.

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP) uses micro-needles to deposit pigment onto the scalp, creating the look of a fuller head of hair. The procedure is non-surgical and non-invasive, which makes it a safe option for men and women experiencing hair loss or thinning.

Our practitioners use current tools and techniques so every client gets a natural-looking, long-lasting result. Whether you want to restore your hairline, fill in thinning areas or cover scars, SMP can give you the look you want and the confidence that comes with it.

The process

Three steps, no surprises.

01

Consultation

A trained practitioner assesses your scalp and hair loss pattern, talks through your goals and preferences, explains the procedure in detail and answers your questions. You leave with a treatment plan built for you.

02

Treatment sessions

Tiny dots of pigment are applied in a pattern that mimics your natural hair growth, with colour and density adjusted so the result looks natural. The number of sessions depends on your level of hair loss and the result you want.

03

Aftercare

You get detailed instructions for your scalp, including avoiding direct sunlight and using mild shampoos. No scratching or picking while the pigment settles. Following aftercare closely protects your result.

Your consultation

Let’s talk it through.

Hair loss can be a sensitive and emotional issue. Your consultation is a private conversation to understand the process and decide whether SMP is right for you.

We walk through the process, answer your questions and show you before and after photos of previous clients. Then we design your new hairline together, considering hair density, colour and the style you want.

Is SMP surgery?

No. SMP is non-surgical and non-invasive. Pigment is placed in the scalp with micro-needles.

Who is it for?

Men and women experiencing hair loss or thinning, and anyone who wants to restore a hairline, fill in thinning areas or cover scars.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your level of hair loss and the result you want. Your practitioner sets out the plan at your consultation.

What does aftercare involve?

Avoid direct sunlight, use a mild shampoo, and do not scratch or pick at your scalp while the pigment settles. You get detailed instructions to follow.
